Rule: Suspicious DLL Load From User-Writable Path
Severity: Medium | Risk score: 47
MITRE ATT&CK: Execution (TA0002) -> Shared Modules (T1129)
Runs every: 5m | Additional look-back time: 1m

Custom query (KQL):
----------------------------------------------------------------------
event.code: "7" and
(process.name: "powershell.exe" or process.name: "pwsh.exe" or
process.name: "rundll32.exe" or process.name: "regsvr32.exe") and
(dll.path: *AppData* or dll.path: *Downloads* or
dll.path: *Users\Public* or dll.path: *Windows\Temp*)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Rule bổ sung (tương quan, EID 11): DLL Created In User-Writable or Temporary Directory
Severity: Low | Risk score: 21

----------------------------------------------------------------------
event.code: "11" and
file.extension: "dll" and
(file.path: *AppData* or file.path: *Downloads* or
file.path: *Users\Public* or file.path: *Windows\Temp*)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Tuning đề xuất (xem Chương 6 báo cáo) — chưa kiểm thử lại trên lab:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
event.code: "7" and
(process.name: "powershell.exe" or process.name: "pwsh.exe" or
process.name: "rundll32.exe" or process.name: "regsvr32.exe") and
(dll.path: *AppData* or dll.path: *Downloads* or
dll.path: *Users\Public* or dll.path: *Windows\Temp*) and
not dll.code_signature.subject_name: ("Microsoft Corporation" or
"Google LLC" or "Adobe Inc.")
----------------------------------------------------------------------

False positive examples:
- Legitimate software installers loading DLLs from temp directories
- Administrative PowerShell scripts loading modules from user profiles
- Development/testing tools loading dependencies from AppData
- Auto-updater (OneDrive, Teams, Slack) cập nhật DLL vào %TEMP%
